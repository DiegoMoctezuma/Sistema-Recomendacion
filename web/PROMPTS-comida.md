# Fotografías ilustrativas de comida

Generadas con la herramienta integrada de imágenes. No son fotografías de los productos o restaurantes del dataset. Los PNG están en `img/` y se identifican como imágenes ilustrativas dentro de la app.

+## img/comida-arroz.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: illustrative food photograph for a food-delivery app demo named A domicilio. Create ONE appetizing realistic editorial food photograph, landscape 3:2 frame. Warm natural daylight, tactile uncoated warm cream tabletop, restrained dark green or cobalt blue ceramic accents. Close camera at a flattering 45 degree overhead angle. Food is the clear hero, fills center of frame, beautifully composed with comfortable cropping room. Authentic casual Singapore food setting, honest food styling, natural texture and subtle imperfections, high-end restaurant menu photography. No writing, branding, logos, interface, watermarks, people or hands. These are illustrative category photos, not photos of actual dataset restaurants. Subject: a plate of Singapore Hainanese chicken rice with tender sliced poached chicken, fragrant rice, cucumber slices, small bowl of ginger chili sauce, steam faintly rising.
